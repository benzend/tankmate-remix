# Dosing Calculator Product Sources

Every coefficient in this calculator is derived from publicly available manufacturer dosing instructions or basic chemistry (stoichiometry). Source audit completed February 2026.

## Source Verification Status

- **Manufacturer-verified**: Coefficient confirmed against the manufacturer's published dosing instructions or product concentration data
- **Stoichiometric**: Coefficient derived from known chemical properties (molecular weight, mass fractions)
- **Third-party only**: Coefficient sourced from reef.diesyst.com community calculator — no manufacturer coefficient published. These are noted in the `sourceNote` field

## Coefficient Formula

All products use the formula:

```
dose = (desired_level - current_level) x tank_volume_gallons / coefficient
```

Where `coefficient` = the parameter increase (ppm or dKH) per 1 unit of product (ml or gram) per 1 gallon of tank water.

## Unit Conversions

- **Volume**: 1 US gallon = 3.78541 liters
- **Alkalinity**: 1 dKH = 0.357 meq/L = 17.86 ppm CaCO3
- **Weight**: 1 gram = 0.035274 oz; 1 ml = 0.033814 fl oz
